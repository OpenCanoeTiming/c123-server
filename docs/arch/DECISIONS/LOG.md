# Decision log

Rows are only added, never removed or reordered. Two kinds of row share it:
- a default taken by the orchestrator where the maintainer was not asked first (production plan §7),
  and a decision the maintainer took;
- the ruling history of the contract: one row per contract round, with the question, the case that
  raised it, its evidence class and what it changed. The normative documents state each rule as it
  stands and point here for who ruled what, when and why (`../README.md`, ground rules).

Columns: date · scope · question · default taken · why reversible · cost of reversing. Rows are in
the order they were written; a row recorded later carries the date of what it records. A factual
correction to an earlier row is the one edit made in place, and a later row names it. A decision that hardens into a rule
becomes an ADR.
